Who I Am

I'm Urfiya Libaibe, a 3rd year Computer Science student at Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering, Mysuru — passionate about building AI systems that solve real problems, not just demo well.

What I've Built

ResistAI
A clinical decision-support platform for antimicrobial resistance in India. A 6-step agent pipeline reasons over real 2024 ICMR national resistance data (not model memory) to give doctors AI-grounded, personalized guidance based on each patient's own antibiotic history. Doctor and patient portals, voice input/output, and support for 7 languages spoken across Karnataka make it usable by patients who can't read.
